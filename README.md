**Climate data scientist & statistics lecturer** at Universitas Negeri Medan, Indonesia.
I turn climate data into answers people can act on: how rainfall extremes are changing, what drives them, and where the risk is highest.

#### 🔬 What I work on
- **Climate time series:** Singular Spectrum Analysis (SSA, MSSA, CiSSA) for rainfall and climate signals
- **Climate extremes:** trends and return levels of extreme rainfall, with ENSO and Indian Ocean Dipole influence
- **Climate risk:** spatial clustering and risk zoning for tropical regions
